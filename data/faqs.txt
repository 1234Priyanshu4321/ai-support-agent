Returns Policy:
Customers can return products within 7 days of delivery.
Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Shipping Information:
Orders are shipped within 24 hours.
Delivery typically takes 2 to 4 business days.

Payment Methods:
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking.
Cash on delivery is not supported.

Refunds:
Refunds are processed within 5 business days after return approval.

Order Cancellation:
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placing them.
After 1 hour, cancellation is not guaranteed as the order may already be packed.
To cancel, go to My Orders and click Cancel Order.

Damaged or Defective Items:
If you receive a damaged or defective item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery.
We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at your choice.
Attach photos of the damage when contacting support.

Delivery Tracking:
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS.
Tracking updates every 4 to 6 hours.
If tracking is not updating for more than 24 hours, contact support.

Exchange Policy:
Exchanges are allowed within 7 days of delivery for size or colour issues.
The item must be unused and in original packaging.
Exchange shipping is free for the first request.

Promo Codes and Discounts:
Only one promo code can be applied per order.
Promo codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.
Expired promo codes cannot be reactivated.

Account and Login Issues:
If you cannot log in, use the Forgot Password option on the login page.
For account lockouts after 5 failed attempts, wait 30 minutes or contact support.
To update your email or phone number, go to Account Settings.

COD (Cash on Delivery):
We do not support cash on delivery at this time.
All payments must be made online at checkout.

Late Delivery:
If your order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, wait one additional business day.
If still not delivered, contact support with your order ID for a status update.
Compensation for late delivery is handled on a case by case basis.

Invoice and GST:
GST invoices are generated automatically for all orders.
Download your invoice from My Orders > Order Details > Download Invoice.
For B2B GST claims, enter your GSTIN at checkout.

Multiple Items in One Order:
Items in the same order may ship separately if they are from different warehouses.
Each shipment will have its own tracking link.

Wallet and Store Credits:
Store credits are applied automatically at checkout if available.
Credits expire 6 months from the date of issue.
Credits cannot be transferred to another account or converted to cash.
